Zero-Knowledge Cryptography

Your data is encrypted before it ever leaves your fingers.

SecuroSpace implements client-side cryptographic isolation. When you enter a task, budget entry, or password, it is derived using your private Master Passphrase and encrypted locally via the browser's native Web Cryptography API.

Local Key Derivation (PBKDF2)

Your master passphrase is never transmitted over the wire. It derives a 256-bit AES key locally using 600,000 PBKDF2 HMAC-SHA256 iterations.

Authenticated Encryption (AES-GCM)

Every encrypted item contains a cryptographic authentication tag, preventing tampering or silent data corruption.

Zero Knowledge Guarantee

Even if our servers or domain host were compromised, an attacker would see only random, unreadable ciphertext. We cannot read your records, sell your data, or comply with decryption demands.

Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about our privacy design, cryptography, and domain operations.

What makes SecuroSpace fundamentally different from Notion, Todoist, or 1Password? +

Most tools either focus solely on passwords or solely on notes, and virtually all of them store your unencrypted habits, financial figures, and daily agendas on centralized cloud databases where they can be scanned, indexed, or breached. SecuroSpace is a unified Life OS built on zero-knowledge foundations: your four core pillars (Tasks, Habits, Cashflow, and Passwords) are encrypted locally before touching storage.

Where is my data stored, and can SecuroSpace see it? +

By default, your data never leaves your browser’s local storage. When you enable encrypted sync, only AES-256-GCM ciphertext is transferred. We do not possess the cryptographic keys to decipher your records. We cannot view your tasks, read your passwords, or analyze your budget.

What happens if I lose my Master Passphrase? +

Because SecuroSpace employs authentic zero-knowledge cryptography, there is no back-door "Forgot Password" reset button. We provide you with an Emergency Kit containing your cryptographic recovery phrase when you initialize your vault. Keep this emergency key in a secure physical location or password safe.

Does SecuroSpace work completely offline? +

Yes! The entire core application is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA) with client-side execution. You can add tasks, complete habits, balance expenses, and generate passwords without an internet connection.

How is securospace.com secured? +

The domain securospace.com is deployed with enforced HTTPS, TLS 1.3 encryption, automated certificate renewals via GitHub Pages infrastructure, and strict Content Security Policies.

Can I export or migrate my data at any time? +

Absolutely. You have 100% data sovereignty. In the app settings, you can export your entire database as an encrypted snapshot or as an open, standard JSON document with a single click.
