SecuroSpace Privacy Policy

We built SecuroSpace on a foundational promise: Your personal life, schedules, financial records, and credentials belong exclusively to you. We do not monetize your data, sell ads, or log your personal habits.

The Core Commitment

We cannot sell, share, analyze, or leak what we cannot see. Because SecuroSpace operates with zero-knowledge client-side encryption, your plaintext personal data is unreadable to anyone but you.

1. Information We Collect (And What We Never Collect)

What We Never Collect:

  • Your Tasks & Focus Records: Plaintext descriptions, notes, and priority levels are processed solely on your local device.
  • Your Habit Tracking Data: Daily routines, streaks, and completion frequencies are stored locally in your browser.
  • Your Financial Ledger Entries: Expense and income figures, transaction descriptions, and net balances are never sent to external servers or banks.
  • Your Passwords & Credentials: Encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM. We never see your Master Passphrase or passwords.

Information Collected Only Upon Explicit Consent:

  • Demo Requests & Early Access Waitlists: When you submit our Book Demo or Early Access forms, we collect your name and email address solely to contact you regarding platform invitations.
  • Voluntary Correspondence: Emails sent to hello@securospace.com or privacy@securospace.com to resolve customer support questions.

2. Cookies & Local Storage Technology

SecuroSpace uses zero third-party tracking cookies, advertising beacons, or surveillance scripts.

We utilize strictly functional HTML5 localStorage within your own web browser to store your encrypted workspace data locally. This data remains on your machine and is never transmitted to advertisers or analytics networks.

3. Zero-Knowledge Architecture & Third Parties

Unlike traditional software companies, our technical architecture mathematically enforces privacy:

  • All encryption occurs client-side via the browser’s native Web Cryptography API before any storage.
  • We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade user lists or personal information to data brokers.
  • In the event of a government subpoena or legal warrant, we can only provide encrypted, unreadable binary ciphertext without the corresponding master keys.

4. Data Sovereignty & Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your jurisdiction, SecuroSpace extends full data sovereignty rights to every user:

  • Right of Access: You can view all stored data at any time inside the app interface.
  • Right to Portability: You can download a complete, standard JSON snapshot of your data with one click via App Settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can wipe all local records instantly using the "Wipe All Local Data" button.

5. Domain & Network Security

All communications on https://securospace.com/ are protected by TLS 1.3 encryption, ensuring that network eavesdroppers cannot inspect or modify payloads in transit.

6. Contacting the Privacy Officer

If you have questions, inquiries, or feedback regarding our privacy architecture, please reach out to:
